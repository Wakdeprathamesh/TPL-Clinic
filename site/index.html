The specialty

Platelet
Rich Plasma

A small volume of your own blood, separated in-clinic into its platelet-rich fraction and reintroduced where regeneration is needed. No donor material. No synthetic filler. The signal your tissue already responds to, concentrated.

STEP 01
Draw

A standard blood draw, taken by a medical practitioner in the treatment room.

STEP 02
Separate

Centrifuged to isolate platelets and growth factors from the remaining components.

STEP 03
Activate

Prepared as plasma, fibrin biofiller or exosome-enhanced formulation for the protocol.

STEP 04
Reintroduce

Delivered by injection or microneedling — frequently alongside RF or laser the same day.
