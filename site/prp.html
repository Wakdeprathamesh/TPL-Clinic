What it is

The signal your tissue already responds to, concentrated.

PRP is a non-surgical, regenerative treatment that uses components of your own blood — making it a natural option with minimal risk of reaction. Platelets, once concentrated and activated, release at least seven key growth factors that stimulate collagen production, improve blood supply, reduce inflammation and encourage cellular renewal.

Results build gradually as tissue responds — which is why PRP is planned as a course, and why it pairs so well with energy-based treatments in the same sitting.

Four steps. All of them yours.

In clinic · single visit
STEP 01
Draw

A standard blood draw, taken by a medical practitioner in the treatment room.

STEP 02
Separate

Centrifuged to isolate platelets and growth factors — concentrations of up to 18× baseline, adjusted to your blood work.

STEP 03
Activate

Prepared as plasma, fibrin biofiller or an exosome-enhanced formulation, depending on your protocol.

STEP 04
Reintroduce

Delivered by injection or microneedling — frequently alongside RF or laser the same day.

Questions

Asked before every first session.

Anything unanswered — call 020 7998 7077 or message us on WhatsApp.

On the face, discomfort is minimal — a topical anaesthetic cream numbs the area first. Scalp injections can be more noticeable, so we use specific tools to keep treatment as comfortable as possible; every pain threshold is different.

Most people need 3–4 sessions, spaced roughly 4–6 weeks apart. Your practitioner sets the protocol against your skin or scalp condition — and for hair, against your TrichoComp™ analysis.

Skin texture and tone improve within about three weeks; full collagen regeneration takes up to three months. For hair, reduced shedding is noticeable within eight weeks, with regrowth typically visible 3–6 months after the final session.

Mild to moderate swelling and redness for 12–48 hours is normal, occasionally with light bruising. Because PRP uses your own blood, the risk of allergic reaction is minimal.

In most patients, results last up to 24 months after a course of four treatments. Touch-up sessions every 3–6 months maintain the effect.

Routinely — that's the clinic's philosophy. PRP is layered with Morpheus8®, exosomes, micro-needling and mesotherapy, frequently in the same sitting, because combination protocols outperform stand-alone treatments.
