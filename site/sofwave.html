What it is

Going beyond just collagen.

From your mid-20s, collagen production naturally slows — fine lines appear, firmness fades. Sofwave™ works beneath the surface, using synchronous ultrasound beams to stimulate collagen, elastin and hyaluronic acid in the mid-dermis — restoring smoother, firmer, healthier-looking skin.

It is completely non-invasive, with no downtime — and many clients see a noticeable improvement after a single treatment, with results building for up to a year.

The TPL protocol — Surf & XERF™

This or that?
Why choose — have both.

Most clinics position Sofwave™ or XERF™ as the "better" treatment. We see it differently. They work at different depths, on different tissue responses — Sofwave™ rebuilds collagen in the mid-dermis; XERF™ delivers deeper monopolar-RF remodelling for jawline definition and laxity.

Layered strategically, 6–8 weeks apart, the combination achieves rejuvenation neither modality reaches alone.
