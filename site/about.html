The founder — 02

Mr Kam Dogan

Founder, Director & Lead Practitioner

Kam graduated from the University of Westminster in Biomedical Sciences in 2014, then spent four years at The Doctors Laboratory and UCL Hospitals. An interest in haematology turned into PRP research during his first Master's degree.

He moved into aesthetics in 2017, motivated by the gap between what the science showed and what the industry was offering. He trains and manages every member of staff, and owns the clinic's quality and safety standards.

He is currently reading Dermatology in Clinical Practice — a second Master's, and a third university degree.
